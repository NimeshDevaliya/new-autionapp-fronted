MedianV Auction Admin Guide

How to set up a season in the admin panel — tournament, franchises, player pool — and then run the live player auction from the console. Every screen below is a real screenshot from the app.

Admin URL http://localhost:3000 API on port 3005. Use your deployed URL in production.
Super admin (dev seed) admin@medianv.com Password Admin@12345 — change before going live.
Auction operator (dev seed) operator@medianv.com Password Operator@12345 — can run auctions.
Money units Everything is entered in lakhs 100 L = 1 Cr. Season 4 franchises ran on a 200 L (2 Cr) purse.

1

Sign in

Page: /login

  1. Open the admin URL. You land on the sign-in screen.
  2. Enter your Email and Password.
  3. Click Sign in. You are taken to the Dashboard.
2

Create the tournament

Page: Tournaments in the left nav

A tournament is one season. Teams and the auction are both attached to it, so create it first.

  1. Click Tournaments in the left nav.
  2. Click + New tournament (top right).
  3. Fill in the form — only the name is required, but series and season details help group seasons across years.
  4. Click Create tournament. The new season appears as a card in the list.
FieldWhat to enter
Tournament name *Full name, e.g. MedianV Premier League Season 5
Short nameUsed where space is tight, e.g. MPL S5
Series name / Season name / Season numberGroups seasons of the same league — MedianV Premier League / Season 5 / 5
LocationVenue city, e.g. Ahmedabad, Gujarat
Start date / End dateEnd date cannot be before the start date
StatusUpcoming / Ongoing / Completed — default Upcoming
DescriptionOptional notes: format, participating teams
3

Create the teams

Page: Teams in the left nav

One team per franchise. Set the purse and squad limits here — the auction console enforces them during bidding. Repeat for every franchise in the season.

  1. Click Teams in the left nav.
  2. Click + New team.
  3. Enter the team details (table below). Pick the Tournament you created in step 2.
  4. Click Create team. The team card shows its purse and player count.
  5. Repeat for each franchise.
FieldWhat to enter
Team name *Franchise name, e.g. Sample Strikers
Short nameUp to 8 characters; shown on scorecards and the auction board, e.g. SSK
OwnerWho runs the franchise
Team colourHex colour for the crest and purse bar, e.g. #f5a524
Purse (lakhs) *Total budget in lakhs. 200 = 2 Cr (Season 4 purse); 1000 = 10 Cr — default 1000
Max playersSquad ceiling — default 18
Min playersSquad floor; must not exceed Max — default 11
Tournament *The season this team plays in — must match the auction's tournament
StatusActive / Inactive. Inactive teams cannot bid — default Active
4

Register players

Page: Players in the left nav

Players are a shared pool — they are not tied to a tournament. A player only needs to be registered once, then can be entered into any auction.

  1. Click Players in the left nav.
  2. Click + Add player.
  3. Enter the player's details. Role and base price are what matter in the auction.
  4. Leave Active — available to be entered into auctions ticked.
  5. Click Add player. Repeat for every registered player.
FieldWhat to enter
Full name *e.g. Arjun Mehta
Role *Batter / Bowler / All-rounder / Wicket-keeper
CategoryLocal / International — default Local
Base price (lakhs) *Opening bid when the player comes up — default 20
Batting style / Bowling stylee.g. Right-hand bat / Right-arm medium
Date of birthOptional
Profile image URLFull https:// link to a photo, or blank
External profile URLCricinfo, Cricbuzz or CricHeroes link, or blank
ActiveTicked = can be added to an auction pool. Untick to bench a player
5

Create the auction

Page: Auctions in the left nav

The auction holds the bidding rules. Increments and squad limits are enforced by the server — the console never trusts the browser with the arithmetic.

  1. Click Auctions in the left nav.
  2. Click + New auction.
  3. Name it and choose the Tournament from step 2 — the same one the teams are attached to.
  4. Set the squad sizes, timer and bid increments (table below).
  5. Click Create auction. It appears as a Draft card.
FieldWhat to enter
Auction name *e.g. MPL Season 5 Auction
Tournament *The season being auctioned — decides which teams can bid
Maximum squad sizeA team cannot buy beyond this — default 11
Minimum squad sizeSquad floor for the season — default 0
Bid timer (seconds)Countdown per player. 0 = no countdown — default 30
Bid increments: IncrementHow much each bid rises by at first — default 1 L
Bid increments: AboveThreshold where the bigger step kicks in — default 40 L
Bid increments: Rises byStep size above the threshold — default 2 L
With the defaults, bidding on a 10 L player goes 10 → 11 → 12 … → 40, then 42 → 44 → 46 and so on.
6

Build the player pool

Page: an auction card → Players

The pool is the list of players who will come under the hammer, in order. Only active players who are not already in the pool are offered.

  1. On the auction card, click Players.
  2. Click Add players.
  3. Search or filter by role, then tick every player to include. The button counts your selection.
  4. Click Add N players.
  5. The Auction order list is the order players come up. Use the trash icon to remove a player before they are sold.
  6. When the pool is ready, click Open console.
7

Run the auction live

Page: an auction card → Console

The console has three columns: the player under the hammer (left), the bid figure with Sell / Unsold (centre), and the teams that can bid (right). Every action is a single click.

  1. Click Start auction (top right). The badge turns Live.
  2. Click Next player. The first player in the order comes up with their Opening bid = base price.
  3. To record a bid, tap the team's card in the Teams panel. Its purse and the current bid update; the team is marked Leading.
  4. Keep tapping teams as they outbid each other. The Next bid figure shows what the next tap will cost.
  5. When bidding stops, click Sell — the player goes to the leading team at the current bid, and the team's purse and squad count update.
  6. If nobody bids, click Unsold.
  7. Click Bring up next player (or Skip to next player) to continue. Use Queue to pick a specific player out of order.
  8. Use Pause / Resume for breaks — bids are blocked while paused.
  9. When done, click End and confirm. Players still in the queue stay unsold.
8

Check the results

Page: Results in the left nav, or the Results button on the console

  1. Click Results.
  2. Choose the auction in the Auction dropdown; filter by team or role if needed.
  3. Switch between the Sold, Unsold and Team-wise tabs.

Bidding rules the server enforces

You cannot break these from the console — the button simply greys out and shows why.

Opening bid = base price

The first tap on a team records the player's base price. Every later tap adds the increment for the current level.

Tiered increments

Bids rise by the first increment until they pass the threshold, then by the larger step.

Not enough purse

A team whose remaining purse is below the next bid is greyed out.

Squad full

A team that has reached its max players (or the auction's squad cap, whichever is lower) cannot bid.

Leading

The team already holding the high bid cannot bid against itself.

Sell needs a bid

Sell stays disabled until at least one bid is on the player. Use Unsold otherwise.

Paused = no bids

While paused, team cards are disabled. Resume to continue.

Ending is final

Once ended, no further bids. Anyone still in the queue is left unsold.

If something looks wrong

Teams panel is empty in the console

The teams are attached to a different tournament than the auction. Open each team → Edit → set the Tournament to match the auction's.

A team card is greyed out

Read the small label on the card: Not active, Squad full, Not enough purse or Leading.

Sell is disabled

No bid has been placed yet. Tap a team first, or mark the player Unsold.

A player is missing from "Add players"

Either the player is not Active (Players → Edit → tick Active) or they are already in this auction's pool.

"Reconnecting" next to the auction name

The live update connection dropped. The console keeps polling every 5 seconds, so it still works — it just refreshes a little slower until it reconnects.

Wrong player came up

Click Queue and pick the right one, or Skip to next player. A skipped player with no bids is left pending, not unsold.
