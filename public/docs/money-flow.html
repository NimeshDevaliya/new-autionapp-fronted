01

The flow, player by player

Every player in the pool goes through the same seven beats. The money only moves at beat six.

1Comes upNext player in the auction order appears on the console.
2Opening bid= base price, 10 L. Nobody has bid yet.
3First bidA team bids 10 L and becomes the leader.
4Counter-bidsEach new team adds one step: 11, 12, 13 … 40, 42, 44 …
5HammerBidding stops. Operator calls Sold or Unsold.
6Purse debitedFinal bid leaves the winning team's purse. Squad +1.
7Next playerRepeat until the pool is empty or the auction is ended.

↻Beats 3–4 repeat as long as teams keep bidding. A team's Next bid is always shown on the console before they tap.

02

How the price climbs

Bids move in fixed steps — nobody types an amount. Below 40 L each bid adds 1 L; at 40 L and above each bid adds 2 L.

Why it matters: reaching 40 L takes 30 bids from base; every bid after that costs 2 L. In Season 4, 9 of 44 auctioned players crossed 40 L, and the top price was 84 L — 22 bids past the 40 mark.
03

Three ways a player leaves the block

Sold

Goes to the leading team at the current bid. Purse, squad count and the player's team update together — all or nothing.

Unsold

No team bid. Nothing is deducted. The player stays registered without a team and can be brought back in a later round or season.

Retained

Kept by the team before the auction; never comes under the hammer. Counts toward the 11-player squad. Season 4 had 16 retained players.

05

When a team cannot bid

The server checks every bid. On the console the team's card greys out and shows the reason — there is no way to overspend by mistake.

SituationWhat the console showsWhat it means
Next bid is more than the purse leftNot enough purseWith 15 L left a team cannot bid 16 L. It can still bid on a cheaper player.
Squad already at 11Squad fullPurse left is irrelevant — the team is out of the auction.
Team already holds the high bidLeadingYou cannot bid against yourself. Wait for another team to counter.
